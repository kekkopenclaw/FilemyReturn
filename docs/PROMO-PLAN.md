## Shot list (what to film / capture)

1. **The mess (0:00–0:03)** — Phone camera, top-down on a desk. Real paper pile, real receipts. No stock needed. 3 seconds.
2. **The scan (0:03–0:08)** — Phone camera, hand holding a phone over a receipt. If the app's mobile upload isn't ready, film the phone scanning with the camera, then cut to the desktop upload.
3. **The OCR demo (0:08–0:16)** — OBS screen recording of the real DocuArchive app, uploading a receipt. Capture the upload, the extracted fields appearing, and the categorised result. No acting, just the app working.
4. **The filing (0:16–0:24)** — Same screen recording: documents appearing in Receipts / Invoices folders.
5. **The search (0:24–0:33)** — Screen recording: type "plumber" in search, result appears. One take, no cuts, so viewers trust it's real.
6. **The lock (0:33–0:40)** — Free stock clip (Pexels) of a locked filing cabinet or a padlock, 2 seconds, overlaid with the search results.
7. **The end card (0:40–0:45)** — Canva free template, plain text: "DocuArchive — Scan it. Done." plus the live link (add once the site is live).

B-roll backup (if the app isn't live on filming day): Pexels free clips of paper, desks, scanning, filing cabinets. Never present stock as the app.

## 2. Free tools — what, what it's free for, exact steps

### CapCut (free) — editing
- Free for: cutting, captions, text overlays, transitions, free music, free text-to-speech, exporting 1080p, no watermark on desktop.
- Steps: 1) Import phone clips + OBS recording. 2) Cut to the timings above. 3) Auto-captions on (most people watch muted). 4) Add the end card. 5) Export vertical 9:16, 1080x1920.

### OBS Studio (free, open source) — screen recording
- Free for: everything, no watermark, no time limit.
- Steps: 1) Open DocuArchive in the browser. 2) OBS → Sources → Window Capture → browser window. 3) Record the upload → OCR → search flow in one take. 4) Save as MP4, import into CapCut.

### Pexels + Pixabay (free) — stock video
- Free for: commercial use, no attribution required, no account needed to download.
- Steps: 1) Search "messy desk paper", "filing cabinet", "padlock". 2) Download 2–3 short clips. 3) Use only as B-roll — never as the app demo.

### Francesco's own voice (free) — voiceover
- Free, and his preferred style (he asked about adding his own voiceover before).
- Steps: 1) Record on the phone in a quiet room, reading the script above. 2) Import into CapCut. 3) If a line sounds off, re-record just that line. No studio needed.
- Backup if he doesn't want to record: CapCut's free text-to-speech, pick a natural British English voice, paste the script in.

### Canva (free tier) — end card + thumbnail
- Free for: templates, text, shapes, export as PNG/JPG.
- Steps: 1) New design 1080x1920. 2) Plain background, big text "DocuArchive — Scan it. Done." 3) Export, use as the final 5 seconds and as the video thumbnail.

### Audacity (free, open source) — audio clean-up, optional
- Free for: noise removal, levelling.
- Steps: only if the voice recording has background hiss — import, Effect → Noise Reduction, export, import into CapCut.

Total cost: £0. Nothing here needs a card.
