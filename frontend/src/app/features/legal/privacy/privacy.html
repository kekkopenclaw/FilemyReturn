Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

DocuArchive is a secure document digitisation and archive management service. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, where it lives, and what you can do about it. We collect only what the service needs to work.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you register, and a hashed version of your password — the plain password is never stored.
  • Your documents: the files you upload, the text extracted from them by OCR, and the details pulled out of them (vendor, date, total, currency and category), plus the tags, filing rules, metadata edits and physical box locations you add.
  • Activity records: an audit log of actions in your workspaces (uploads, views, downloads, edits, deletions and sign-ins), kept so you can see what happened to your documents.
  • Service health data: system-wide totals used by administrators to run the service — numbers of users, workspaces and documents, storage used, and failed sign-ins in the last 24 hours. These are aggregates only; they contain no document contents.
  • Cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a refresh-token cookie. That is all — there are no tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics.

2. How we use it

We use your data only to run the service for you: to sign you in, to store, read, classify, file and search your documents, to enforce workspace membership and roles, to keep the audit trail, and to keep the service secure and running. We do not use your documents or their contents for any other purpose, and we do not use them to train any model.

3. Where your data lives

Your account, documents and extracted text are stored on the service's own server — a database (SQLite on the hosted service, PostgreSQL as an option for self-hosted installs) and file storage on that server. Documents are read by OCR running on the same server: they are never sent to an outside AI service, and no document contents leave the server except to your own browser when you view or download them.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties except where the law requires it.

4. Who can see it

Your documents are visible only to you and to the members of the workspaces they belong to, according to their roles. A member of one workspace cannot see another workspace's documents. A system administrator can export a backup of the whole archive and can see the system-wide totals described in section 1, but does not browse your documents as part of running the service.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual documents and whole workspaces at any time from inside the service. When you ask for your account to be closed, your personal data is deleted; audit records of past actions may be kept briefly for security purposes. Backups made by an administrator contain a snapshot of the archive at the time they were taken, and are kept by the administrator under the same rules.

6. Cookies

The service uses two cookies, both strictly necessary: a session cookie that keeps you signed in while you use the service, and a refresh-token cookie that renews your session. Both are HttpOnly, which means page scripts cannot read them. There are no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies.

7. Your rights

You can view, correct and delete your documents and workspaces inside the service at any time. You can ask the site operator for a copy of the personal data held about you, or for your account to be closed and your personal data deleted (see section 9). If you believe your data has been mishandled, you can raise it with the site operator, and with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the service develops. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Contact the site operator: contact details to be added by the site owner.
